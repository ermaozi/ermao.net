As of June 1, 2026, several users had reported abnormal website behavior, unusable subscriptions, and ineffective communication channels at Koodog. The available evidence indicated a high likelihood of shutdown. Existing users were advised to stop renewing or adding credit and preserve account and payment records.

::: warning Evidence limitations
This assessment is compiled from public screenshots and user reports. Some important details remain unverified.

Until the platform issues an official explanation and restores service, treat it as high risk.
:::

## Website and communication status

* Website: Users reported that `https://zero.thisgourl.xyz/#/register?code=BSkBAzZz` automatically redirected to a Baidu search page.
* Client and subscriptions: Multiple reports said that subscriptions were unusable and the client could not connect normally.
* Support: Users reported that tickets did not work or were not handled, but the status of the support system's backend remained unverified.
* Official announcement: No clear public explanation was found at the time of the report.

## Shutdown-risk analysis

### Timeline based on available evidence

* April 23, 2026: A user reported that service quality had deteriorated significantly.
* June 1, 2026: A user reported abnormal website behavior, including a redirect to Baidu.
* June 1, 2026: The group received concentrated outage reports, with several users saying that subscriptions were unusable.

### Evidence classification

* Confirmed from the submitted material: Multiple screenshots show access problems and unusable subscriptions.
* High-probability warning signs: Several users reported problems at the same time, spanning the website, subscriptions, and tickets.
* Still unverified: Whether the operator issued a maintenance or migration notice, and whether support staff remained active.

## Risk conclusion

The visible evidence shows problems across several independent areas: website access, subscription availability, and communication.

Without a clear operator explanation or restored service, this page's current assessment is: **highly suspected shutdown**.

This wording reflects the evidence available to the source article; it does not claim that every remaining official channel was independently checked.

## Advice for users

* Stop renewing and adding credit immediately.
* Export and back up subscription URLs, plan records, payment evidence, tickets, and conversations.
* If a working ticket or community channel remains, submit a written refund or explanation request and preserve screenshots.
* Move primary use to an alternative service to reduce single-provider exposure.
