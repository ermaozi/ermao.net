## Latest CAC update (July 1, 2026)

An important connection in the source evidence is that **CAC, Naiyun, Yinyun, and OKAC share the same operator**.

CAC's problem therefore should not be treated only as an isolated website failure. Naiyun's shutdown has been confirmed, and OKAC experienced website, node, support, and management-response problems during the same period. Service and financial risks among projects under the same operator should be assessed together.

## Analysis of CAC's shutdown risk

::: caution

CAC's website became inaccessible, and the service shares an operator with Naiyun, Yinyun, and OKAC.

Stop purchasing and renewing to avoid further losses.

### Late June 2026: CAC website inaccessible

Submitted material showed that CAC's website could not be accessed around late June 2026. In isolation, that could be a temporary website failure. In combination with Naiyun's confirmed shutdown, OKAC's concurrent problems, and the common operator, it warranted a shutdown alert.

### Concurrent problems among services under the same operator

Naiyun's shutdown has been confirmed. Around the shutdown, users reported unreachable support, failed nodes, an inaccessible website, and invalid subscription URLs. From mid-May, OKAC experienced repeated A-route and multi-route failures, data loss and order restoration, website-access problems, and interrupted management responses.

## Conclusion

The available evidence shows these risk factors:

* CAC's website was inaccessible.
* CAC shares an operator with Naiyun, Yinyun, and OKAC.
* Naiyun's shutdown is confirmed, and OKAC has developed problems across several operational areas.
* No verifiable CAC-specific recovery announcement or migration plan had been found.

This site currently classifies CAC as a **provider shutdown risk**.

## Loss-mitigation advice

* Stop renewing and adding credit immediately.
* Back up and export subscription URLs, plan details, payment records, tickets, and conversations.
* If the account dashboard or ticket system still works, request an explanation or refund in writing and preserve screenshots.
* Monitor Naiyun, Yinyun, and OKAC as related risks; do not treat a service under the same operator as an independent backup.
