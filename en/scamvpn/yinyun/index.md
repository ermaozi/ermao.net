## Analysis of Yinyun's shutdown risk

::: caution

Yinyun shares an operator with Naiyun, CAC, and OKAC. Naiyun's shutdown has been confirmed, and several services under that operator have developed problems.

Stop purchasing and renewing to avoid further losses.

:::

### The common operator is the basis of this alert

The source article identifies Naiyun, CAC, Yinyun, and OKAC as services under the same operator. For users, this indicates that operations, support, funds, and route resources may be closely connected.

When several of those services develop problems at the same time, the risk should be treated as an operator-wide issue rather than a single provider's incidental outage.

## Conclusion

The current alert is based on:

* Yinyun sharing an operator with Naiyun, CAC, and OKAC;
* Naiyun's confirmed shutdown and simultaneous website, node, or support problems among several projects under the operator;
* no unified, verifiable recovery explanation found for the related projects;
* the increased financial exposure created by renewing or adding credit.

This site currently classifies Yinyun as a **provider shutdown risk**.

## Loss-mitigation advice

* Stop renewing and adding credit immediately.
* Back up and export subscription URLs, plan details, payment records, tickets, and conversations.
* Do not use Naiyun, CAC, or OKAC as an independent backup for Yinyun.
* If Yinyun itself develops website, node, subscription, or support problems, preserve screenshots promptly and submit the additional evidence.
