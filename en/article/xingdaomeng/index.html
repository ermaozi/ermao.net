Frequently asked questions

Are traffic multipliers used?

The provider says no. Verify the current node list because policies can change.

Are devices limited?

No count limit is advertised under reasonable use. Confirm simultaneous-session and fair-use definitions before household or team sharing.

Are refunds supported?

Consult the current website terms or support. The source does not provide a confirmed refund window.

Before you order

  • Resolve the IEPL-versus-IPLC route wording.
  • Confirm quota, multipliers, device rules, reset or expiry date, discounts, and refunds.
  • Test evening peaks and your specific ChatGPT, TikTok, and streaming regions.
  • Do not prepay for several years merely to obtain a discount.
  • Keep a backup route for business or important account work.

Conclusion

Xingdao Meng offers a low CNY 16 entry tier, high-traffic monthly plans, and one-time traffic. Its routing, access, device, speed, and support points are primarily provider statements. The sensible approach is a small monthly test with explicit checks of the route terminology and services you need.
