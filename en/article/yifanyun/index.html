Purchase guidance

  1. Verify the current registration domain and promotion.
  2. Start with VIP1 monthly.
  3. Confirm client import, data reset, devices, concurrency, and refund rules.
  4. Test the evening peak, weekend, and required services for 3–7 days.
  5. Treat the undocumented one-time plan as unavailable until its price and rules are visible.
