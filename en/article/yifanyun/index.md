## Compatibility conflict

The source lists Shadowrocket, Clash, v2rayN, v2rayNG, Surge, Quantumult X, and Stash on iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS, while also saying universal subscriptions are unsupported.

This can mean the provider supplies client-specific imports rather than one portable universal URL. Confirm the exact workflow for every intended device before purchase.

## Purchase guidance

1. Verify the current registration domain and promotion.
2. Start with VIP1 monthly.
3. Confirm client import, data reset, devices, concurrency, and refund rules.
4. Test the evening peak, weekend, and required services for 3–7 days.
5. Treat the undocumented one-time plan as unavailable until its price and rules are visible.

## FAQ

### Is Yifan Cloud beginner-friendly?

The plans are simple, but client portability is limited. A beginner should confirm the official import path first.

### Which plan should be tested first?

VIP1 at CNY 30 per month.

### What must be checked before payment?

Subscription compatibility, reset rules, refund terms, and the current discount.
