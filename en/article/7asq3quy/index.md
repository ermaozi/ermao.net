On February 11, 2025, SFCloud and FCCloud were confirmed shut down. Their websites were inaccessible, all servers were offline, and support tickets went unanswered.

**Stop renewing immediately to avoid additional losses.**

At the time, ~~XSUS~~ stated that it could accept SFCloud and FCCloud users at 30% of their previous subscription value and asked them to submit a support ticket after registering on the ~~XSUS website~~.

::: warning
XSUS itself triggered a shutdown risk alert on April 28, 2025 and is not recommended.
:::

When choosing a proxy service, a longer operating history may reduce—but cannot eliminate—shutdown risk. The source article used [Dageyun](https://ermao.dgywzc.com/#/register?code=peAVAa8D) as one example.

:::

## Initial assessment

SFCloud and FCCloud originally received traffic from EFCloud. EFCloud shut down on January 25, 2025, making an SFCloud and FCCloud shutdown highly likely at that point.

Related article: [EFCloud Shutdown—January 25, 2025](/en/article/nz313xp6/)
