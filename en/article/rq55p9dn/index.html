KooDog Review—Archived CNY 5 Entry Tier and 2 Gbps Claim

About 423 wordsAbout 1 min

proxy-service reviewKooDogTrojanstreaming

...

2025-08-23

KooDog was described as a newer Trojan-based proxy service with dedicated routes for three mainland access carriers. The source claimed a peak single-thread result of 2 Gbps and almost 100% availability, but supplied no repeatable test method or uptime series.

Recorded route: KooDog registration

Provider-stated features

The source lists:

  • Windows, iOS, macOS, and Android clients;
  • one-click connection and remote technical assistance;
  • Trojan protocol subscriptions;
  • an Emby media library; and
  • a purported no-login Netflix access benefit.

Treat the bandwidth, availability, dedicated-route, and streaming statements as provider or source claims. A no-login streaming arrangement can disappear, expose a shared account, or conflict with the streaming service's rules.

The original page advertised a Mid-Autumn/Qixi promotion using code koodog.com through September 15. Because the article was created in August 2025, that promotion is historical and should not be assumed active.

Archived tests

Screenshots capture one historical condition. They cannot establish a general 2 Gbps rate or almost-perfect availability.

Purchase boundary

Verify the current official domain, plan, route, refund policy, and support channel. Begin with the smallest monthly tier and test the destinations you need. Do not base a long prepayment on the expired discount or streaming-account benefit.
