Archived test

One screenshot and plan information cannot establish long-term or evening-peak stability. Test from your own ISP at midday, evening peak, and late night for several days.

Compatibility and access boundaries

The provider material mentions mainstream media and AI tools but includes no complete node-level access matrix or repeat tests.

No universal subscription means the buyer must accept the provider's client or specific import path. Confirm the app publisher, signature, supported platforms, update route, permissions, and migration options.

Purchase guidance

  1. Resolve whether a CNY 10 monthly tier actually exists.
  2. Prefer the lowest documented monthly plan, CNY 15 for 50 GB.
  3. Confirm the provider client and support route.
  4. Test speed, interruptions, and required websites for 3–7 days.
  5. Treat the undocumented one-time plan as unavailable.
  6. Do not prepay a long term while operating history is short.

FAQ

Is Kuaili suitable for a beginner?

Only if the provider client supports the user's device and the lack of universal subscriptions and Telegram is acceptable.

Which plan should be used first?

The lowest table-documented monthly plan is CNY 15 for 50 GB. The CNY 10 claim needs checkout confirmation.

What is the main risk?

A short operating record combined with client lock-in and limited public support information.
