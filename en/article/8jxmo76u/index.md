## Who might consider it?

* A light user whose measured monthly use is near or below 100 GB.
* A beginner testing a monthly plan instead of prepaying for a year.
* A user who already has a primary connection and wants an independent backup.

Frequent 4K video, large downloads, or several users can consume 100 GB quickly. Measure actual client traffic before choosing a larger plan.

## Checks before purchase

1. Begin with a monthly plan or trial.
2. Confirm import into the actual Clash, Shadowrocket, or other intended client.
3. Test evening-peak behavior, required regions, and required services on the actual broadband connection.
4. Save tickets and payment records.
5. Maintain a backup from another operator.
