⚠️ EFCloud Shut Down on January 25, 2025

About 681 wordsAbout 2 min

censorship circumventionVPNproxy servicereview

...

2025-01-25

On January 25, 2025, EFCloud shut down. Its website was inaccessible and its subscriptions no longer worked. The shutdown has been confirmed, and this site no longer recommends the service.

At the time, XSUS offered to take over EFCloud users and transfer part of their remaining subscriptions. Users were directed to the Telegram group @xsusvpn for details.

Warning

XSUS triggered a shutdown risk alert on April 28, 2025. This site does not recommend using XSUS.

Risk-monitoring board

Many proxy services have recently become unreachable after data centers removed their servers. Consult the 2025–2026 Proxy-Service Shutdown and Risk List for additional records.

Official announcement

Screenshot

English translation

Dear EFCloud users,

Hello. I am a friend of Diaomao. At this difficult moment, I am writing with deep gratitude and great reluctance.

First, I sincerely thank everyone for supporting and trusting EFCloud along the way. Every use, word of encouragement, and expression of support gave us motivation to continue. That is why, when the Hong Kong landing node recently expired and failed, I paid part of the cost myself so that your service would not be interrupted while Diaomao was absent.

Unfortunately, I still have not been able to contact Diaomao. As more servers gradually expire, I can no longer keep the service running on my own. I do not have sufficient permissions to repair or manage the problems, so EFCloud will have to stop operating. This is a decision I very much did not want to face, but it is the present reality.

To reduce users' losses and continue their service, I have decided to migrate existing users to XSUS so that their trust can be carried forward. https://xsus.one/

  1. Users whose plans are still valid can migrate to XSUS free of charge.
  2. Users whose plans are about to expire, or who do not currently have a valid plan, can receive 20% off a renewal with the code 20OFF202501.
  3. I will also process recent outstanding affiliate withdrawals as soon as possible, and affiliates are welcome to continue working with XSUS.

This was not an easy decision. I understand what EFCloud means to many users: it is not only a service, but also a set of memories and a source of companionship. I therefore hope that migrating to XSUS can keep your experience from being interrupted as much as possible. If EFCloud can return in the future, you will still be welcome to come back and continue that journey.

For account-migration arrangements, contact @xsus_support_bot.

If you have any questions or concerns, please let me know and I will do my best to resolve them. Group: @xsusvpn

The migration may cause inconvenience, for which I am deeply sorry. I also ask for your understanding: this difficult choice was made to prevent greater losses wherever possible.

Finally, thank you again to every user who accompanied us. Although EFCloud is drawing to a temporary close, I hope XSUS can become a new beginning that carries your expectations, and that this new journey can continue to bring warmth to your lives.

May every day ahead bring us more light and beauty.

Respectfully,
A friend writing with gratitude and regret

The text above is a faithful translation of the operator-side announcement shown in the screenshot. Its claims about migration, discounts, and affiliate withdrawals are statements from the announcement, not independently verified promises from this site.
