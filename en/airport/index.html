How to choose

First purchase

Prefer monthly billing or a trial. Avoid annual, multi-year, or lifetime plans until the service has passed your own tests.

Stability

Check what IEPL, IPLC, or BGP labels actually mean, then review evening-peak tests and the provider history.

Client flexibility

Prefer a universal subscription compatible with the clients you already trust, and confirm router support before paying.

Provider risk

Search this site for closure records and confirm that the website, subscriptions, tickets, and support channels still work.

Price

Compare traffic, multipliers, device limits, speed caps, and reset dates instead of looking only at the headline price.
