### Clear monthly data tiers

Existing records list 150 GB, 300 GB and 600 GB monthly options. Starting small lets you choose your next allowance from actual account usage.

A monthly allowance can leave unused data when your usage varies. The recorded 1,000 GB and 3,000 GB packages instead work against a total allowance.

**No expiry means the package data is not cleared at a monthly deadline.** It does not mean unlimited data or guaranteed permanent service. Check whether a package can coexist with a recurring subscription and how switching affects the remaining allowance.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Which plan should a first-time buyer compare?

Start with Satellite monthly, recorded at CNY 18 for 150 GB. Its smaller initial payment lets you check your connection, client and usual websites before choosing the next allowance.

### How does the annual small plan differ from no-expiry data?

The annual plan requires a full-year payment, has a fixed service term and supplies monthly allowances. A no-expiry package consumes a total allowance without monthly clearing. Confirm the actual validity and switching rules before buying.

### Does it support Clash and Shadowrocket?

The inspected access page provides related import options. Confirm the exact client version, subscription format and plan compatibility in its guides; the presence of an import option is not proof that every device or version has been tested.

### Are ChatGPT, Netflix or high-resolution video guaranteed?

This article has not independently tested those services. Check your own device, node and usual time of use. A data allowance alone cannot guarantee a particular number of video hours.
