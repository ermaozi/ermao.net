## Who might test it?

The CNY 18 monthly tier may suit someone evaluating a new service or secondary route. The service is a poor fit for anyone who requires a mature operating record, documented service levels, or a refund window.

## Before purchasing

1. Confirm current data, multiplier, devices, reset, and discount at checkout.
2. Read the no-refund rule in its current written form.
3. Buy only the smallest monthly tier.
4. Test the evening peak, node switching, required regions, AI services, and streaming catalogs over several days.
5. Keep an independent backup for work, livestreaming, stores, or valuable accounts.

## FAQ

### Is Huanyu Cloud beginner-friendly?

The standard subscription and low entry cost may be approachable, but the short operating history makes it an observation candidate rather than a proven choice.

### Which recorded plan minimizes risk?

The CNY 18 monthly tier exposes less money than annual or one-time plans. The CNY 89 plan is paid annually even though its monthly data is small.

### What matters most before payment?

The non-refund rule and one-week operating history. Verify both before relying on a promotional price.
