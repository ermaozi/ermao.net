“Non-expiring” describes the data balance, not the provider, account, domain, or node lifetime.

Provider-stated features

Routes and capacity

The provider describes all routes as enterprise IPLC/IEPL, advertises up to 2.5 Gbps of capacity, and says load balancing optimizes ingress for China Telecom, China Unicom, and China Mobile. It also claims low latency, very little packet loss, and strong evening-peak performance.

The source does not contain route contracts, complete traces, capacity graphs, or a controlled multi-day test. A 2.5 Gbps provider figure is not a promise that one account or node can sustain that rate.

Regions and node counts

The source lists 20 Hong Kong nodes, 10 Japan nodes, 10 United States nodes, and more than 60 nodes in total, with additional locations across Asia and Europe. Check the current dashboard: node counts and availability can change independently of this article.

Device policy

The provider advertises no simultaneous-device limit, allowing phones, computers, tablets, and routers on one account. “Unlimited devices” may still be constrained by fair-use, sharing, IP-count, concurrency, or account-security rules. Read the current terms before using it with a family or team.

Streaming and AI access

Provider materials and the source test report Netflix, Disney+, TikTok, Hulu, ChatGPT, and YouTube access. They also use “native IP” and “full access” language. Platform classification changes over time, so test the exact catalog, region, account, and AI feature required.

Support

The source reports continuous human support and a ticket system. Submit a small pre-purchase question to observe response quality, and verify every support identity from the authenticated dashboard.

Frequently asked questions

Does JiLianCloud's route label prove high stability?

No. The IPLC/IEPL label is a claim to validate with current route evidence, evening-peak latency and loss, and application tests on the intended ISP.

Can the coupon be combined with a long-term discount?

The source says it can, but stacking rules may change. The checkout total is the controlling value. Avoid a two- or three-year commitment based only on a larger percentage discount.

Is it suitable for gaming?

Possibly, if the node's route to the exact game server performs well. Test latency, jitter, loss, and route stability during the hours played. Streaming speed tests do not answer that question.

Which payments were listed?

Alipay and USDT. Use only the payment destination shown in the authenticated dashboard and understand the refund implications.

What should I do if it does not connect?

Refresh the subscription, verify the plan has data, synchronize system time, test another node, and check local networking. Then use the provider's verified ticket channel.

Before purchasing

  • Confirm the current operator, official domain, and support route.
  • Check plan, allowance, reset, multiplier, device, sharing, and refund rules.
  • Reproduce performance on the intended ISP and during the evening peak.
  • Test the exact game, streaming catalog, or AI feature required.
  • Prefer a small monthly tier over annual or multi-year prepayment.
  • Keep an independently operated backup for important work.
