Advertised advantages

  • Plans from a low-cost entry tier to a 600GB tier.

These are provider descriptions. Route type, uptime, access, and device policy should be checked in the current terms.

Clients and usage

Runway uses Shadowsocks and can be imported into compatible clients:

  • Android: Clash for Android or v2rayNG
  • iOS: Shadowrocket or Stash
  • Windows: Clash Verge Rev or v2rayN
  • macOS: Clash Verge Rev or ClashX

Possible starting choices:

  • Light browsing or a backup: Economy.
  • Regular primary use: Business, after testing its speed cap.
  • High traffic or many devices: First Class, after confirming fair-use rules.
  • Intermittent use: the one-time traffic package, after confirming expiry.

How to buy

  1. Open the Runway registration page.
  2. Register and complete any email verification.
  3. Choose Economy, Business, First Class, or the one-time package.
  4. After payment, obtain the subscription URL from the account dashboard.
  5. Import it, test several nodes, and select the best route for your connection.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest plan?

Economy was listed at CNY 9.90 per month with 100GB.

Which protocol and clients are supported?

The service uses Shadowsocks. Compatible options include Clash for Android, Shadowrocket, Clash Verge Rev, and v2rayN.

Is there a one-time package?

Yes. The price table listed 150GB for a single CNY 45 payment.

Who might consider Runway?

Its price tiers may interest budget-conscious beginners, streaming users, or people who need a larger traffic allowance. Suitability still depends on tests from the user's own network.

Before you order

  • Confirm traffic, speed limits, device rules, reset cycle, discounts, and refund terms at checkout.
  • Buy a small or short plan first and test evening peaks, ChatGPT, streaming, and your usual regions.
  • Keep a backup connection for work, livestreaming, commerce, or account operations.

Conclusion

Runway's main attraction is a CNY 9.90 entry plan and clearly separated traffic and speed tiers. Its streaming, peak-hour, route, uptime, and unlimited-device statements remain provider claims. It is a test-first option rather than a reason to prepay for a long term.
