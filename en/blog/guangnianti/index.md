## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Does Guangnian Ti offer a refund guarantee?

The provider says users may contact support for a refund when a line problem cannot be resolved. This article has not independently verified the time limit, exclusions, or procedure, so read the current refund terms first.

### Is there a simultaneous-device limit?

The entry plan is advertised without a client limit. Other plans may also be permissive, but confirm any fair-use policy and simultaneous-connection limit before sharing an account.

### Are all nodes billed at 1x?

That is the current provider claim. Multipliers affect how quickly your quota is consumed, so recheck the node list after purchase.

### Is evening-peak performance guaranteed?

No. The provider promotes its IEPL lines and smooth 4K playback during 20:00–23:00, but real performance depends on your connection and current load.

## Before you order {#risks}

* Do not decide from the headline price alone. Recheck traffic, multipliers, device limits, reset cycle, and discounts on the payment page.
* Start with a small or monthly plan. Test evening peaks, ChatGPT, YouTube or Netflix, and the regions you use before committing for longer.
* Keep a backup connection for work, streaming, store operations, or account management. Node maintenance, congestion, and routing changes are normal operational risks.
