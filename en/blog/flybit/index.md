These are useful attributes to test, not guaranteed outcomes. Begin with the smallest monthly tier and verify evening-peak performance on the actual ISP.

## Provider claims to verify

* IEPL routes across the listed nodes;
* access to Netflix, Disney+, YouTube Premium, ChatGPT, and Bing AI;
* no device limit;
* high availability across recently added nodes;
* stable evening-peak performance.

No route label alone proves a private-line contract. Third-party platforms can change IP and regional policies, and an “unlimited devices” statement may still have simultaneous-connection or fair-use conditions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Has Flybit shut down?

The source reported normal operation and recently added nodes on March 2, 2026. That is a dated observation, not a current uptime guarantee. Check the provider's independently verified announcement channel, domain, and support response before payment.

### Is it a good value?

The CNY 15 entry price is low relative to the recorded 128 GB allowance. Value still depends on route quality, peak-hour behavior, node availability, and whether the required applications work from the user's network.

### Why is a node not working?

1. Check whether the plan is active and has remaining data.
2. Refresh the subscription through the trusted client.
3. Confirm that system time is synchronized.
4. Test ordinary local connectivity.
5. Try another node and read the provider status notice.

## Purchase checks

* Verify traffic, multiplier, device count, reset cycle, coupon, and refund terms at checkout.
* Test a small monthly tier for several days before prepaying.
* Check ChatGPT, YouTube or Netflix, required regions, DNS, and evening-peak behavior separately.
* Keep an independently operated backup for work, livestreaming, stores, and important accounts.
