## Historical profile

![Xiaoniu Cloud website =593x564](https://image.ermao.net/images/blog/xiaoniu/image.png)

The author had used the service for more than six months and reported a positive experience during that period. Later node problems and reports of unresponsive support changed the purchasing assessment. Historical performance does not establish current availability.

The source recorded these earlier provider claims:

* more than 140 nodes;
* dedicated-route positioning;
* Shenzhen Telecom, Mobile, and Unicom entry points;
* a Shenzhen–Hong Kong route from an unnamed large provider;
* egress hosting from OVHcloud, ZEN-DPS, M247 Europe, BlueVPS, PQ Hosting Plus, Ultahost, and others;
* Netflix, Disney+, and ChatGPT access; and
* a CNY 2.99 entry test.

The named infrastructure and node count were not independently documented. The earlier support contacts were [AoxvpnVIP](https://t.me/AoxvpnVIP) and [aoxcloud](https://t.me/aoxcloud), but a contact link is not proof that support remains responsive.

## What existing customers should do

1. Disable automatic renewal.
2. Save the plan, order, payment recipient, remaining term, and dashboard.
3. Archive subscription and error information privately.
4. Save support conversations and outage timestamps.
5. Prepare an independent replacement service.
6. Do not add more money merely to recover remaining data.

## FAQ

### Is Xiaoniu Cloud currently recommended?

No long-term purchase is recommended. This page is primarily a historical and incident-reference record.

### Why preserve prices and client links?

Existing customers may need to identify a plan, troubleshoot a subscription, or migrate. Historical details are not an invitation for new payment.

### Does the author's earlier positive experience cancel the warning?

No. A service can work for months and later become unreliable or close.
