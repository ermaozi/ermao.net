Civet Network Review—CNY 25 Monthly, IPLC Claim, and Conflicting Protocol Details

About 791 wordsAbout 3 min

2026-07-28

Civet Network reportedly began operation in 2026. Its lowest ordinary monthly plan was CNY 25 for a labelled 150 GB, while a CNY 85 annual small plan was labelled 45 GB. The submitted material does not say whether these allowances reset monthly, apply once per billing period, or use another schedule.

Recorded purchase route: Civet Network plans

Conflicts and provider claims

The provider advertises IPLC, 1x data deduction, no speed or device limit, universal subscriptions, multi-person support, and an overseas technical team.

The submitted details conflict in several places:

  • marketing mentions a "new SS protocol," while both screenshots label every node Vless;
  • one payment description says USD and another says USDT;
  • claims of CNY 6 or CNY 7 per month conflict with the explicit plan table; and
  • data-reset timing is absent for every allowance.

The translation preserves these conflicts rather than choosing an unsupported answer.

Service-access claims

The provider says it supports mainstream streaming, ChatGPT, Gemini, and TikTok, with common nodes in Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan, the United States, and Singapore plus other Asian, Indian, Korean, European, and American regions.

The supplied access test covers only YouTube, Netflix, and Disney+ on five common regions. It does not test ChatGPT, Gemini, TikTok, or the other region claims.

The source also says a Shadowrocket download account can be supplied. Shared app-store accounts create credential and recovery risk. Prefer an account you control and never store payment information in a supplied account.

Archived service-access test

The MiaoKo screenshot is labelled July 19, 2026 at 18:18:02 CST and "Zhuhai Unicom 9 Gbps, 32 threads." It shows 50 VLESS nodes, TLS RTT around 66–246 ms, HTTPS latency around 348–1,003 ms, and region-labelled access for YouTube, Netflix, and Disney+.

Archived speed test

The speed image is labelled July 19 at 17:24:44 CST. Displayed averages range about 7.74–545.80 MB and peaks about 13.78–633.53 MB. UDP results include Full Cone, Unknown, and Restricted Cone.

Both images are provider-supplied single tests on the same day. Device, client, user location, access ISP, independent baseline verification, and repeated time periods are missing.

Purchase guidance

  1. Start with Monthly Small, not an annual tier.
  2. Confirm the actual protocol and standard-subscription format.
  3. Resolve whether foreign payment is USD or USDT.
  4. Obtain the data-reset, device, concurrency, and refund rules.
  5. Test required platforms and the evening peak from your own network.
  6. Keep an independent backup.

FAQ

What is the lowest clear monthly price?

CNY 25 for the Monthly Small plan. The CNY 85 plan is annual and does not establish a CNY 6 or CNY 7 month-to-month option.

Is a universal subscription supported?

The provider says yes, but the actual protocol conflicts with the marketing text. Verify the dashboard output.

Are ChatGPT, Gemini, and TikTok verified?

No. The supplied access test does not show those services.

Are devices and speed unlimited?

Those are provider claims. No independent concurrency or traffic-policy test was supplied.

Is there a one-time plan?

The submitted material does not say, so no conclusion can be drawn.
