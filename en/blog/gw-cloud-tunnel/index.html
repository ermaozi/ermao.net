The three-year operating history is a provider submission; no exact start date or continuous operating record was independently verified. The supplied payment methods are Alipay and WeChat Pay. Begin with the lowest monthly amount and retain the order and plan terms shown at purchase.

Who may consider it

  • Users who want to test universal-subscription compatibility with a CNY 15 monthly tier.
  • Individuals whose monthly allowance needs fall between 100 GB and 1,000 GB.
  • Buyers who will consider the 1,300 GB annual or 1,500 GB one-time tier only after validating the route.
  • Users who accept that platform access changes by node and retain an independent backup for important work.

For a first purchase, avoid the annual and one-time tiers. Repeat tests on your normal ISP, devices, evening-peak periods, and target platforms before increasing the prepaid amount.

FAQ

What is the lowest GW Cloud Tunnel plan?

The lowest recurring tier in the supplied material is CNY 15 for 100 GB per month. One text field says 150 GB, but the plan screenshot says 100 GB, which this review uses. Verify the live checkout.

Are universal subscriptions supported?

The submission says yes. The test labels its nodes as Shadowsocks, but that does not prove every plan or generated subscription contains only that format. Confirm compatibility with your exact client.

Is the CNY 100 for 1,300 GB tier a no-expiry plan?

No. The screenshot says it is valid for one year, so this review classifies it as an annual traffic tier. The no-stated-expiry tier is CNY 260 for 1,500 GB, usable until depleted according to the provider.

Does the test prove long-term stability?

No. It is a single provider-supplied node-latency and platform-check image without a complete environment, date, repeated measurements, or download-throughput results.

How long has the provider operated?

About three years according to the submission. The exact start date was not independently verified. Prefer short billing periods and keep an independent backup.

Conclusion

GW Cloud Tunnel lists four monthly tiers—CNY 15 for 100 GB, CNY 20 for 300 GB, CNY 35 for 600 GB, and CNY 50 for 1,000 GB—plus a CNY 100 annual tier with 1,300 GB and a CNY 260 one-time tier with 1,500 GB. The submission says universal subscriptions are supported, while the provider's screenshot shows multi-region Shadowsocks nodes and platform checks. Neither replaces repeated testing in the buyer's environment.

The lower-risk path is to start with the CNY 15 monthly tier, verify subscription compatibility, common nodes, and target platforms, and only then consider a higher-traffic or prepaid plan.
