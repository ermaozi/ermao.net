## Advertised features {#section-3}

### BGP return routing and dedicated nodes

The service promotes BGP routing and dedicated nodes across Hong Kong, Singapore, the United States, and Japan to reduce jitter and latency. Route labels alone do not establish actual performance.

### Low congestion

The provider claims stable latency and limited congestion. Verify this during your own evening peak and over several days.

### Cross-platform support

The service advertises iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and router compatibility, plus an account dashboard for node and traffic status. Plan-specific device limits still apply.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### How fast is it?

The plans list caps between 500Mbps and 5,000Mbps. Those caps are not guaranteed throughput. Test on your own connection.

### Does it support Netflix?

The provider advertises multi-region Netflix and other media access across all plans. Access can change by node and platform policy.

### Are one-time packages available?

Yes: 100GB for CNY 28 and 288GB for CNY 68 in the source table. Confirm account-expiry rules.

## Before you order {#risks}

* Recheck quota, speed cap, multiplier, device limit, reset or expiry cycle, and discounts.
* Begin monthly or with a small one-time package.
* Test evening peaks, ChatGPT, YouTube or Netflix, and common regions.
* Keep a backup route for work, livestreaming, commerce, or account operations.
