## Benefits and boundaries

Potential benefits:

* a low initial monthly tier;
* a simple four-level data structure; and
* provider apps that may reduce setup work.

Important limits:

* no mature operating record;
* no universal subscription according to the source, which restricts client portability; and
* no public Telegram group or channel recorded at the time.

An "unlimited speed" label does not guarantee throughput. IPLC and IEPL labels should be tested on the specific nodes included with the selected plan.

## Purchase strategy

Begin with White Cat for one month. Confirm that the provider client supports your operating system and can be removed cleanly. Test evening-peak latency, interruption recovery, required AI services, and streaming regions. Maintain an independent backup for consequential work.

## FAQ

### Can a beginner use Ermao Cloud?

The provider app and simple plans may be approachable, but it remains a new service. Do not treat it as an established provider.

### Which plan should be tested first?

White Cat at CNY 20 and 100 GB. Move to a larger tier only after validating stability and support.

### What is the largest lock-in concern?

The source says universal subscriptions are not supported. Users who require a trusted third-party client should choose another provider or confirm that this has changed.
