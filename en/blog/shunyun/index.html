Shunyun Review—CNY 99 Annual Plan and Anycast Service Claims

Shunyun is positioned as a moderately priced multi-node service for streaming and daily work. Recorded plans range from a CNY 99 annual small tier to a CNY 68 monthly 600 GB tier.

Recorded website: ddd.jichang.best

The source also claims entry points for China Telecom, Mobile, and Unicom; overseas maintenance; strong evening-peak performance; and compatibility with Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, and routers. None of those claims includes an independent test or service-level record.

Every plan is described as using mainstream-country Anycast nodes. Verify the actual node set, traffic multiplier, devices, personal-use rule, and refund policy at checkout.

The annual tier has the lowest average monthly cost but the CNY 20 Traveler tier requires less initial payment and gives a direct monthly test.

Anycast and access boundaries

Anycast can route one destination address to different network locations, but the label alone does not establish the physical egress region, dedicated capacity, or equal performance from every ISP.

The provider claims every node supports Netflix, Disney+, TikTok, ChatGPT, and other platforms. Service access can change with egress IP, account, region, and platform policy. Test the exact requirement rather than a home page.

Purchase process

  1. Open the recorded registration route.
  2. Prefer Traveler for one month over the annual headline plan.
  3. Confirm subscription format, devices, reset, multiplier, and personal-use limits.
  4. Import the URL into a trusted compatible client.
  5. Test the evening peak and required regions for one or two weeks.
  6. Upgrade only after measuring actual data use.

USDT is generally irreversible. Do not use it merely because it is offered.

FAQ

What is the lowest recorded price?

CNY 99 for a full year with 59 GB resetting each month. The lowest month-to-month tier is CNY 20 for 150 GB.

Are streaming and ChatGPT supported?

The provider says they are. The source provides no independent access test.

Which payment methods were listed?

Alipay, WeChat Pay, and USDT.

Is support available all day?

The provider claims 24/7 human support through tickets and online service. No response-time evidence was supplied.

Purchase boundary

Shunyun can be considered for a one-month test, but node count, Anycast, access, support, and performance remain provider claims. Keep an independent backup for work, livestreams, stores, and valuable accounts.
