### Streaming and AI services

Provider materials claimed access to Netflix, Disney+, TikTok, TVB, and ChatGPT across all plans. They also used a “native IP” label and linked it to lower account risk.

IP labels are not standardized guarantees. Streaming catalogs, AI features, and risk controls can vary by address, account, node, and time. Test the exact region and workflow needed, and avoid relying on a proxy address to make a valuable account “safe.”

### Support and onboarding

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the CNY 99 annual plan the best choice for a beginner?

Its monthly equivalent is low, but the user prepays a full year. A small monthly plan gives better evidence about the operator, support, routes, and peak-hour performance before taking annual-payment risk.

### Which plan should a heavy streaming or AI user choose?

Estimate actual monthly data first, then test the CNY 17 monthly tier on the required services. Upgrade only after checking node quality, reset and multiplier rules, and current checkout prices.

### Is the old 20% coupon still valid?

The recorded campaign ended on January 31, 2026. Treat `ok88` as expired unless the current checkout explicitly accepts it and shows the reduced final amount.

### What matters most before payment?

Resolve the domain difference, verify the operator and refund terms, and reproduce performance on the intended ISP during the evening peak. Marketing statements about dedicated lines, native IPs, capacity, and guaranteed continuity are not substitutes for that evidence.

## Before purchasing

* Confirm the live plan, allowance, reset, multiplier, device, and refund rules.
* Do not evaluate the annual plan only by dividing its price by 12.
* Start with a monthly tier and test several days before prepaying.
* Verify every required service and region rather than relying on a compatibility list.
* Keep an independently operated backup for work, streaming, or account operations.
