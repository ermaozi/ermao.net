Lumo Review: Basic and Premium Routes, Plans, and Traffic Multipliers

About 1321 wordsAbout 4 min

2026-10-27

Lumo separates its routes into Basic and Premium groups. The submitted pricing starts at CNY 29/month for Plus with 150 GiB/month among monthly options. Lite costs CNY 119/year with 365 GiB/year. These allowances use different periods: Lite's annual total is not a monthly allowance. Compare route multipliers and client compatibility as well as the headline price.

Basic and Premium accounting

The supplied Basic policy lists 0.6× from 01:00 to 19:00 and 1.0× from 19:00 to 01:00. Its timezone was not specified. A CST timestamp on a test screenshot does not establish the billing-policy timezone.

Premium is marketed as Gomami / DMIT connectivity. That label does not independently establish a circuit contract, dedicated capacity, or superior performance. Both screenshots label Premium entries 2.8×. Confirm the current multiplier and whether time-based variations apply. Inclusion in every plan does not imply identical accounting to Basic.

Compare connection success and application response on your own access network, then consider the allowance consumed. Do not treat the Premium label as a performance guarantee.

Features and compatibility

The provider describes self-operated DNS, subscription authentication and auditing, node monitoring, and load balancing, and claims support for Netflix, ChatGPT, and Gemini. These have not been independently tested here and are not a guarantee for every node, region, or account.

The public client notice lists clients and configuration reminders. It does not prove that arbitrary older versions support AnyTLS. Check the required client, core version, and subscription format before importing. Keep subscription URLs private: they contain access credentials.

Registration and setup

  1. Open the supplied Lumo entry and check the current page and announcements. We did not register or sign in; these are general steps, not verified dashboard button labels.
  2. If a trial is offered, read its duration, allowance, and limits before testing the regions and applications you need.
  3. Choose a plan after checking reset periods, multipliers, device and concurrent-use limits, renewal, and refund rules. Those terms are not all available in the supplied evidence.
  4. If eligible, verify that hellolumo reduces the amount before payment. Do not assume an undisplayed discount will be refunded later.
  5. Follow the dashboard's current subscription instructions with a compatible client. Test both groups and inspect allowance deductions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the lowest submitted price?

Lite is CNY 119/year for 365 GiB/year. Plus is the lowest monthly option at CNY 29/month for 150 GiB/month. Verify the live checkout and keep the different periods distinct.

Does every plan include Premium?

The submitted information says yes, but the screenshots label Premium 2.8×. Verify current access and accounting rather than assuming all routes consume data at 1×.

Is Netflix or AI access guaranteed?

No. These are provider claims, not independent findings here. Availability may change with the node, account, region, and platform policy.

Should a new customer pay annually immediately?

An annual price does not replace testing. Check trial or monthly experience, refunds, data periods, and multiplier rules before accepting longer prepayment.

Lumo's relevant differences are its two route groups, accounting multipliers, and annual versus monthly allowances. Check current plans and trial conditions before deciding on a term.
