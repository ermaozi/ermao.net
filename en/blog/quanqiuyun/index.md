## Provider-stated features

### Dedicated-route and service access claims

The provider describes native datacenter routes, enterprise IPLC/IEPL, intelligent load balancing, and three-carrier entry optimization. It says nodes are labelled for Netflix, Disney+, TikTok, ChatGPT, and other services. Test the exact node, catalog region, AI feature, and account operation required.

### More than 70 nodes

The advertised list includes Hong Kong, Japan, Singapore, the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, South Korea, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, and Turkey. The provider also claims independent IP resources, more than 3 Gbps of bandwidth, and roughly 500 Mbps of peak-period redundancy.

Node presence, upstream capacity, and usable subscriber throughput are different facts. The source did not independently verify every location or capacity figure.

### Client compatibility

The supplied material labels the protocol VLESS and says the service works with Shadowrocket, Clash, V2Ray-family clients, iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and routers. It also says one account can be used on multiple devices. Confirm the exact subscription format, device count, and simultaneous-connection rules for the purchased tier.

### Support

The provider advertises online support, tickets, and an overseas technical team. Response time and maintenance quality were not independently verified; test support with a pre-purchase question and retain the answer.

## Before purchasing

1. Verify live pricing, allowance resets, account validity, device rules, VLESS subscription format, and refunds.
2. Begin with the CNY 20 monthly tier rather than an annual, high-volume, or one-time plan.
3. Test evening-peak behavior, required regions, and exact streaming or AI features.
4. Ask for written route, bandwidth, IP, service-term, and incident-handling details before buying a private node.
5. Keep a separately operated backup for work, livestreaming, stores, or important accounts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a beginner use it?

Yes, but the CNY 20 monthly tier is the sensible first test. Verify the VLESS subscription, local ISP, target nodes, and intended services before considering an annual, high-volume, or one-time plan.

### Which plan is appropriate?

Use the 120 GB monthly tier for a first test. Consider 300 GB after measuring regular video or AI traffic, and 700 GB or more only after validating the route and service. One-time packages suit infrequent backup use but create more prepayment exposure.

### What matters most before checkout?

Check the current price, data reset, account validity, node regions, device rules, subscription format, and support entry point. For the private node, obtain the route, bandwidth, IP ownership, service term, and fault-handling terms in writing.
