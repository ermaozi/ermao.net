  • access to ChatGPT and other external AI services;

Encryption does not make the operator unable to observe connection metadata, and no node can guarantee continued access to a third-party platform.

Who might consider each tier?

  • 120 GB: light browsing, social media, a backup connection, or an initial test;
  • 300 GB: regular video or several personal devices;
  • 600 GB: heavier personal use after measuring real traffic.

The provider's personal-use rule means “ten devices” should not be interpreted as permission to share the account among unrelated people.

Before purchasing

  1. Confirm current device limits, reset schedule, manual-reset fee, multiplier, and coupon at checkout.
  2. Test the smallest monthly tier for several days.
  3. Repeat checks during the evening peak for the required regions and applications.
  4. Maintain a separately operated backup for work, livestreaming, stores, or valuable accounts.
  5. Save the provider announcement, ticket, and payment record.
