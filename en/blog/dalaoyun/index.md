## Before purchasing

* Compare CNY 15 monthly for 50 GB separately from CNY 96 annually for 60 GB/month. Confirm payment total, currency, reset date and expiry.
* Confirm coupon eligibility: the limited CNY 15 tier explicitly excludes coupons.
* Verify your client can import the actual subscription. A VLESS label alone does not establish compatibility, and the submission says universal subscriptions are unsupported.

* Start with an affordable short billing period, test your required platforms and evening conditions, and retain a backup.

## FAQ

### Is CNY 8 a monthly plan?

No. It is the monthly average of the CNY 96 annual payment. The lowest monthly option in the supplied screenshots is CNY 15 for 50 GB. The annual tier provides 60 GB/month, refreshed every 30 days from purchase.

### Are there one-time packages?

The newer images list CNY 268 for 200 GB and CNY 498 for 500 GB total. Unused data is retained without a monthly reset until consumed, replacing the original text saying none.

### Which payments and subscription formats are supported?

The submission lists Alipay, WeChat Pay and USDT and says universal subscriptions are unsupported. We have not independently checked dashboard formats or payment channels.

### Are these our tests?

No. Both archived images are user-supplied records dated 23 September 2026. They include zero speed, failed access, N/A and region differences; neither includes a Claude test.
