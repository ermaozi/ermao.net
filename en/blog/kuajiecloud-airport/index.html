2026 Kuajie Cloud Review: CNY 20 for 120GB and Dedicated-Line Claims

About 1305 wordsAbout 4 min

Proxy ReviewDedicated LineUniversal SubscriptionStreaming

...

2026-07-28

According to provider-supplied material, Kuajie Cloud launched in 2026 and recently upgraded its network to what it calls “all dedicated-line routes.” The lowest recurring tier, Light Cloud Lite, is CNY 20 and lists 120GB. Other tiers list 300GB, 700GB, and 1,500GB with monthly, quarterly, and annual billing.

Official link: View Kuajie Cloud plans

Overview

The provider says existing routes were upgraded to dedicated lines and that existing users can access them without an extra charge until their current plan expires or traffic is depleted. The route type and claimed latency or speed improvement were not independently verified.

  • Launch: 2026, according to the provider
  • Positioning: “all dedicated-line routes,” according to the provider
  • Universal subscription: advertised as supported
  • Payments: WeChat Pay and Alipay
  • Telegram: Kuajie Cloud group
  • One-time plan: marked as available, but no specific product details were supplied

Because the service is new, long-term stability, incident handling, and support continuity remain to be observed. Begin with the lowest monthly tier.

One-time plan

The provider marked a one-time, non-expiring subscription as available but did not supply its name, total traffic, price, account lifetime, or restrictions. This article does not invent a comparison tier.

If one is currently sold, confirm total traffic, whether traffic avoids periodic clearing, account validity, inactivity rules, and treatment if service ends. “No time limit” is not a guarantee of permanent operation.

Main characteristics

Dedicated-line upgrade claim

The provider says the entire network was upgraded and that existing users receive the new routes without extra payment through their current term. It also claims lower latency and higher speed, but supplied no entry-route details, dedicated-line type, bandwidth cap, before-and-after controlled test, or service-level commitment.

Four traffic tiers and three billing periods

Recurring plans range from 120GB to 1,500GB. Higher prices or longer billing do not automatically mean better per-node speed. Evaluate actual monthly use and prepayment risk.

Universal subscriptions and payment

Universal subscriptions, WeChat Pay, and Alipay are listed. Subscription format, compatible clients, device count, concurrency, and refund rules are not.

Reading the supplied tests

Node-speed screenshot

The provider-supplied MiaoKo screenshot is dated July 19, 2026 at 16:18:50 CST. It identifies a 9Gbps China Unicom backend in Zhuhai, 32 threads, and 50 of 50 entries. Fifty VLESS nodes appear across the United States, Japan, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Singapore.

Displayed regional results vary substantially: United States entries average about 10.38–15.89MB/s, while Hong Kong entries show about 424.21–547.67MB/s. Taiwan node TWN03 shows 0.00B. UDP types are mostly FullCone, with Unknown and one RestrictedCone result.

These backend figures are not speeds an ordinary local connection is guaranteed to reach. This is one 9Gbps, 32-thread provider-supplied run without repeat tests across user ISPs, devices, clients, or times.

Streaming screenshot

A second MiaoKo screenshot is dated July 19, 2026 at 16:07:45 CST and lists the same five regions and 50 VLESS nodes. YouTube, Netflix, and Disney+ columns show region-specific access.

That only records the test state at the screenshot time. Platform detection and exit IPs can change. Retest with your account and usual nodes.

Who may consider it?

  • Users willing to test the CNY 20 monthly tier and dedicated-line claim.
  • Users who need 300GB to 1,500GB after confirming the quota basis.
  • Buyers using WeChat Pay or Alipay who will verify device, reset, and refund rules.
  • Users who accept the limited history of a 2026 launch and keep a backup route.

Do not buy an expensive annual tier solely from one backend screenshot.

Purchase steps

  1. Register through the official link.
  2. Prefer the CNY 20 monthly Light Cloud Lite tier.
  3. Confirm price, traffic reset, validity, devices, concurrency, and refunds.
  4. Pay through a currently listed method and retain the order and terms.
  5. Import the subscription and test on your normal network during peak hours.
  6. Only then consider a higher quota or longer billing period.

Frequently asked questions

What is the lowest price?

The provider's table lists Light Cloud Lite at CNY 20 monthly with 120GB. Its reset basis is unspecified.

Is it entirely dedicated-line based?

The provider says the network was upgraded to dedicated lines. This article has not independently verified the route type or before-and-after performance.

Are universal subscriptions supported?

The provider says yes. The exact format, compatible clients, and device count were not supplied.

Is a one-time plan available?

The submission says yes but gives no price, traffic, or validity rules.

What do the test images prove?

They show one July 19, 2026 speed run on a 9Gbps backend and one access check. They do not prove future access, local-user performance, or long-term stability.

Conclusion

Kuajie Cloud launched in 2026 and promotes a network-wide dedicated-line upgrade. Recurring tiers begin at CNY 20 with 120GB listed. Universal subscriptions and common local payment methods are advertised, but quota resets, detailed client policy, and one-time plan data remain unclear. Test monthly before extending the term.
