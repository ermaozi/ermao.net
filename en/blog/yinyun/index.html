Frequently asked questions

Should users buy Yinyun now?

No. Stop new purchases and renewals. Preserve records instead of putting more money into a service under a correlated-operator warning.

What should existing users do?

Back up the subscription and payment evidence, disable automatic renewal, arrange an unrelated backup provider, and monitor the website, nodes, tickets, and official notices.

Why assess Yinyun together with Naiyun, CAC, and OKAC?

The source identifies the same operating entity. Failures across websites, nodes, support, and management under one operator increase correlated risk.

Conclusion

Yinyun formerly promoted dual client modes and server-side routing. Current risk control matters more:

  • Naiyun under the same operator is confirmed closed.
  • CAC and OKAC showed multiple failures.
  • Yinyun is therefore under a correlated provider-exit warning.
  • Users should stop purchases and renewals and preserve evidence.

Do not choose Yinyun as a primary or backup service now. Read the Yinyun provider-exit warning and choose an operator without the same linked risk.
