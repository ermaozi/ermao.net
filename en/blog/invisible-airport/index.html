Purchase guidance

  • Begin with Silver Era for one month.
  • Treat Starlight as 80 GB for the whole year.
  • Confirm the one-time package device rule and account expiry.
  • Reject the custom plan until its service term and billing are explicit.
  • Test target platforms and evening peaks locally.
  • Retain a backup for important work.

FAQ

What is the lowest normal monthly tier?

CNY 24 for 144 GB per month. The CNY 109 plan supplies 80 GB for the entire year.

Is a universal subscription supported?

The source says yes. The test labels nodes VLESS; verify the actual dashboard format.

Are one-time packages available?

The detailed table lists CNY 229/160 GB, CNY 549/420 GB, and CNY 1,199/1,000 GB, despite an earlier conflicting checkbox.

How many devices can use a one-time package?

Detailed rules say one, two, and three devices respectively. The smallest package also has an inconsistent unlimited-device summary, so confirm in writing.

How long has the provider operated?

About two years according to the provider. No exact independently verified start date was supplied.
