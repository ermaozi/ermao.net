# Corrections and Feedback Policy

> Found a price change, broken link, outdated version, or factual error? Please report it. Clear feedback supported by checkable evidence can be corrected more quickly.

::: card-grid cols="3"

- **1. Page URL**

  Include the complete URL of the page containing the problem.

- **2. Specific issue**

  Identify the relevant passage and explain what you believe is inaccurate.

- **3. Checkable source**

  Provide an official page, announcement, dated screenshot, or other primary material.

:::

<LinkCard icon="material-symbols:mail-rounded" title="Email a correction" href="mailto:admin@ermao.net?subject=Website%20content%20correction&body=Page%20URL%3A%0ASpecific%20issue%3A%0ACheckable%20source%3A%0A" description="The email template includes fields for the page URL, the specific issue, and a checkable source." />

## What happens after feedback is received

The site distinguishes factual corrections from differences of opinion and claims that cannot be verified. If an error that affects a conclusion is confirmed, the article will be revised and its modification date updated. A brief note will be retained in the article when the change is significant. Fixing only a typo or formatting issue should not cause a page to be presented as substantively updated.

Providers may submit factual evidence, but they may not demand the removal of accurate negative records or exchange a commercial relationship for a changed conclusion. Remove or obscure personal data before sending anything that contains user information, credentials, or subscription details.

::: tip Privacy reminder
Before submitting a screenshot, redact email addresses, order numbers, subscription URLs, access tokens, payment information, and any other personal data.
:::
