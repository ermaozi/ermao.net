Telegram contains a large number of public channels, groups, and other resources, but its built-in discovery features do not cover everything. This guide explains how to use the third-party [JiSou bot](https://t.me/jisou?start=a_1453425105) to search Telegram.

The original Chinese article also noted that some `+86` mobile numbers could register at the time of writing. Registration availability and verification delivery can change, so treat that as a dated observation rather than a guarantee.

<!-- more -->

## Download Telegram

On Windows, you can install Telegram from the [Microsoft Store](ms-windows-store://pdp?launch=true&mode=full&hl=zh-cn&gl=hk&ocid=bingwebsearch&referrer=storeforweb&productid=9nztwsqntd0s&storecid=sfw-secondary-cta&webid=395477ea-a7e6-4bfa-ac47-e06e900153a8&websessionid=9303d2ac-2b4a-48fc-b501-5acf807b00ed).

You can also download an official client from the [Telegram website](https://telegram.org/).

## Registering an account

At the article's April 2025 publication date, Telegram was accepting some `+86` numbers. If a number cannot receive a code or complete registration, an overseas mobile number obtained through an eSIM may be an alternative.

The source article warned that **5ber appeared to have stopped reliably delivering downloadable eSIMs after purchase** and mentioned 9eSIM as an alternative. Verify the current status, device compatibility, refund terms, and the long-term availability of any number before paying. Losing access to the registration number can make account recovery difficult.

## Optional Simplified Chinese language pack

Open the [Simplified Chinese language-pack link](https://t.me/setlanguage/zhcncc) in Telegram.

![Telegram language-pack screen =493x388](https://image.ermao.net/images/article/wwpg9n59/image-2.png)

Select `Apply Language` to install it. This is a community language pack, not a requirement for using Telegram.

## Search with JiSou

After signing in, open the [JiSou search bot](https://t.me/jisou?start=a_1453425105).

![Opening JiSou in Telegram =540x595](https://image.ermao.net/images/article/wwpg9n59/image.png)

Start the bot, enter a keyword, and choose a result to open the corresponding public resource in Telegram.

![JiSou search results =854x917](https://image.ermao.net/images/article/wwpg9n59/image-1.png)

## Safety notice

JiSou is a third-party search service. A search result is not proof that a channel, file, product, or claim is legal, authentic, or safe.

- Follow the laws that apply to you and do not download or distribute unlawful material.
- Do not disclose verification codes, recovery passwords, payment credentials, or identity documents to a bot or channel.
- Treat unsolicited investment offers, paid “account recovery,” and requests to move money as high-risk.
- Review Telegram's privacy settings and enable two-step verification.

If you encounter a problem with this guide, leave a comment with the client and platform you are using.
