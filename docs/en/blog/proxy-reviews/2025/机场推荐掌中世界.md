## Provider-stated features

The original marketing claimed:

- a seven-day free trial;
- 24-hour support;
- simple app-based connection;
- rapid YouTube 8K playback;
- optimization for China Mobile, Unicom, and Telecom 5G networks;
- Netflix access;
- support for phones, computers, and routers; and
- a supplied United States Apple ID with Shadowrocket.

These are provider claims. Video resolution depends on sustained throughput, device, codec, server, and time. A supplied or shared Apple ID creates credential, recovery, purchase, and platform-policy risk; obtain apps through an account you control.

The source suggested the service for streaming, games, and remote meetings. One provider and one historical test cannot guarantee low latency or business continuity for those uses.

## Before using the trial

Confirm whether the trial requires payment details, automatically renews, limits nodes, or collects more data than a normal account. Test:

- your own access network;
- the evening peak;
- the exact streaming region or meeting service;
- interruption recovery; and
- the official client source.

The absence of a text plan table means prices and allowances must be read from the current checkout rather than inferred from screenshots.
