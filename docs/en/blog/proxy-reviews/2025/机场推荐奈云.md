Naiyun is treated as a confirmed provider closure. By July 1, 2026, reports recorded unreachable support, unavailable nodes, an inaccessible website, and failed subscription URLs. A notice in Yinyun's official channel also said Naiyun had closed.

::: danger Stop paying
Do not purchase or renew Naiyun. Save plan, payment, subscription, status, and support records.

Naiyun, Yinyun, CAC, and OKAC are reported as services under the same operator. Do not treat another name under that operator as an independent backup. Read the [Naiyun closure record](/en/scamvpn/naiyun/).
:::

## What affected users should retain

- the order and payment recipient;
- plan and remaining-term screenshots;
- subscription URL in a private evidence archive;
- status and error captures with timestamps;
- support conversations; and
- the official-channel closure notice.

Do not publish active subscription credentials while reporting the incident.

Compare replacement services only after reading the [provider-risk index](/en/scamvpn/jichang-paolu-huizong/) and [proxy-service review list](/en/posts/vpn/).
