## Provider-stated architecture

The source attributes these route types to Zongyunti:

- direct connections to overseas servers;
- relay connections through a domestic entry;
- IEPL-labelled dedicated routes; and
- automatic BGP entry changes during busy periods.

It also claims global server distribution, mainstream subscription-client support, an overseas operations team, self-developed technology, advanced encryption, and stable access to AI services without degraded model behavior.

These are not independently verified. An IEPL or residential-IP label does not establish the physical route, exclusive capacity, privacy, account safety, or stable access to a particular AI model. A cumulative-spend discount should not be a reason to prepay before testing continuity.

## Purchase boundary

Before payment, confirm:

- current operator and domain;
- the smallest monthly plan;
- node classes and traffic multipliers;
- device and sharing rules;
- whether the advertised route applies to every plan;
- refund and support terms; and
- discount validity without increasing the commitment unnecessarily.

Test several nodes during the evening peak and keep an independent backup for important use.
