Chongshang Yunxiao markets a self-developed service platform, Taiwan residential-IP nodes, automatic BGP ingress switching during the evening peak, and routes for multiple mainland access carriers. These are provider claims; this page does not independently prove the underlying route contracts or IP classification.

- Website: [cpdd.one](https://cpdd.one/?r=32083)
- Recorded code: `ermaozi`
- Stated benefit: 20 GB of extra data after purchasing a plan
- Lowest plan recorded in the source: CNY 5 for 80 GB valid for 30 days

Prices, routes, code eligibility, and service availability can change. Verify the current checkout page and start with the smallest monthly purchase.

## Before purchasing

1. Confirm that the intended client can import the subscription.
2. Test the required region and service from the actual access network.
3. Check peak-hour latency, loss, and sustained throughput rather than one screenshot.
4. Treat “residential IP,” “dedicated line,” and automatic BGP switching as claims to verify.
5. Keep an independently operated backup.
