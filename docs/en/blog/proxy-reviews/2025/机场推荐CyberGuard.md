CyberGuard was presented in the source as a multi-platform proxy service with plans beginning at CNY 18 per month. The page contains broad provider claims about speed, privacy, support, and infrastructure but no independent logs or policy evidence.

## Provider information

The source attributes these features to CyberGuard:

- support for Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android;
- servers in several countries and regions;
- an easy-to-use client;
- promotional discounts at unspecified intervals;
- 24-hour customer support; and
- encrypted connections.

These statements should not be treated as verified uptime or privacy guarantees. Encryption does not establish a no-logs policy, operator identity, legal jurisdiction, incident response, or protection from endpoint tracking.
