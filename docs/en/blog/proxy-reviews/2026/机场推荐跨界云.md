## Main characteristics

### Dedicated-line upgrade claim

The provider says the entire network was upgraded and that existing users receive the new routes without extra payment through their current term. It also claims lower latency and higher speed, but supplied no entry-route details, dedicated-line type, bandwidth cap, before-and-after controlled test, or service-level commitment.

### Four traffic tiers and three billing periods

Recurring plans range from 120GB to 1,500GB. Higher prices or longer billing do not automatically mean better per-node speed. Evaluate actual monthly use and prepayment risk.

### Universal subscriptions and payment

Universal subscriptions, WeChat Pay, and Alipay are listed. Subscription format, compatible clients, device count, concurrency, and refund rules are not.
