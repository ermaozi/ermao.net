## Choosing a plan

- Use the CNY 3 tier to confirm that AnyTLS or the provided subscription works with your trusted client.
- Consider Basic only after testing the evening peak and required destinations.
- Choose Pro because measured consumption requires it, not because 1,000 GB looks inexpensive.
- Treat one-time plans as provider credit that lasts only while the service and account remain available.

## Compatibility and risks

AnyTLS requires a compatible current client. A subscription that imports successfully into one provider app may not be portable to another client; the airport catalog records Danke as not supporting a universal subscription.

Before paying annually, verify the provider identity and the written refund rule. Low price and large allowances increase the temptation to prepay but do not reduce continuity risk.

## FAQ

### Can a beginner test Danke?

Yes, if the CNY 3 tier is still available and the user can verify client compatibility. It is a test tier, not evidence of long-term performance.

### Which tier is the reasonable next step?

The CNY 9.90 Basic tier is the lowest recorded regular monthly option. Upgrade only after measuring real use.

### What needs checking at checkout?

Confirm AnyTLS compatibility, whether a standard subscription exists, current plan data, reset and multiplier rules, and how support announces outages.
