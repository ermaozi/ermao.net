## Provider information

The provider claims:

- more than 60 nodes in Hong Kong, Japan, Singapore, the United States, Taiwan, and other regions;
- VLESS and enterprise IEPL routes;
- optimized entry for three mainland carriers;
- proprietary clients for major platforms;
- universal subscriptions for Shadowrocket, Clash, V2Ray, Trojan, sing-box, and routers;
- 2 Gbps or greater independent bandwidth and 500 Mbps peak reserve; and
- access to Netflix, Disney+, TikTok, ChatGPT, and Claude.

These claims have not been independently measured.

## Purchase guidance

- Begin with Basic View for one month.
- Verify both the proprietary app's publisher and universal-subscription import.
- Use the annual keep-alive tier only for genuinely low total use.
- Treat long billing and one-time packages as increased provider exposure.
- Obtain a written specification for the private route.
- Maintain a backup for important work.

## FAQ

### What is the lowest recurring tier?

CNY 25 for 125 GB per month. The separate CNY 89 annual plan provides 60 GB for the year.

### Is a universal subscription supported?

The source says yes and lists several client families. Confirm the live format and platform-specific import instructions.

### Do the one-time packages expire?

The provider says they do not. Confirm account-validity and closure terms before purchasing.

### Are streaming and AI services verified?

No. Those are provider claims, and the supplied speed test does not show access checks.
