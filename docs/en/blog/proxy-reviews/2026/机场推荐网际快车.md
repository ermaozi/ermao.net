## Provider-stated advantages and their limits

### Residential IPs

Residential addresses may encounter different platform treatment from datacenter addresses, but classification databases disagree and a shared residential address can still be risky. Verify the egress with more than one current data source and protect valuable accounts with stable sign-in behavior.

### Non-expiring allowance

This can reduce waste for occasional use because the recorded balance does not reset monthly. It does not reduce operator-shutdown risk; preserve only a modest balance until operating history and support are proven.

### No device-count limit

Confirm whether the rule also permits the intended number of simultaneous connections and whether it is limited to one person or household. “Unlimited devices” is not necessarily permission to resell or share the account.

### Dedicated applications

A dedicated app can simplify onboarding but adds a software-supply-chain decision. Prefer a standard subscription in a trusted client where available, or verify the application's publisher, signature, update source, permissions, and privacy terms.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest package?

The source recorded CNY 6.80 for 20 GB advertised not to expire. Confirm the current checkout amount and minimum payment.

### How many devices are permitted?

Provider materials say there is no device-count limit. Ask separately about simultaneous sessions, fair use, and account sharing.

### Who benefits from a non-expiring package?

An occasional user who needs a backup connection and does not want a monthly reset. A heavy continuous user should compare the actual effective cost and daily restrictions of the time-based plans.

### Does it work with AI services?

The provider and source test report access to several services. Test the exact product feature and account flow. Network access does not guarantee that an account meets a service's supported-country or risk requirements.
