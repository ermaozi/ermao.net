## Provider-stated features

### IPLC routes

Provider materials describe enterprise private-line transport, low latency, no public-internet traversal, and continued availability during sensitive periods. The source does not include carrier documents or a complete route record. The “never disconnects” implication is not supportable as a guarantee.

Use route evidence where visible, then measure loss, jitter, latency, and application behavior on the intended ISP and during the evening peak.

### Streaming, AI, and TikTok

The provider reports access to Netflix, Disney+, YouTube Premium, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and TikTok, and markets the service for international e-commerce and livestream operations.

Access is not the same as operational suitability. Valuable store, creator, advertising, or AI accounts can be affected by IP reputation, geography, sign-in consistency, and platform risk controls. Test the exact workflow and retain an independent backup.

### Device and support policy

The provider advertises no device-count limit and says several customer-service staff work in shifts with support from an overseas technical team. Neither statement is independently measured. Current fair-use and sharing terms can still restrict how an account is used.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the annual entry tier appropriate for a beginner?

Its effective monthly cost is low, but it requires full-year prepayment. A monthly plan provides better evidence about the operator, routes, support, and peak-hour quality before committing longer.

### Can a family or team share one account?

The provider says there is no device-count limit. Check account-sharing, simultaneous-IP, fair-use, and security rules; “unlimited devices” does not necessarily permit unrelated users.

### Is AI and TikTok access guaranteed?

No. The provider reports optimizations, but platform policies and IP classifications change. Test the exact account and workflow, and do not rely on one exit IP for important operations.

### What should be confirmed before payment?

Resolve the two domain references, check protocol compatibility and plan terms, and reproduce evening-peak performance. Also understand the ownership and recovery terms of any supplied Shadowrocket or Telegram account.

## Before purchasing

- Confirm the official domain, operator, and support channel.
- Verify plan, reset, multiplier, device, sharing, and refund rules.
- Treat IPLC, no-throttling, no-limit, and service-access entries as claims.
- Use a monthly tier before taking annual-payment or stored-balance risk.
- Test the actual ISP, region, application, and evening peak.
- Keep an independently operated backup for work or account operations.
