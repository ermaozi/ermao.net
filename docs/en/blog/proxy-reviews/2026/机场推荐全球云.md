## Provider-stated features

### Dedicated-route and service access

The provider describes native datacenter routes and low latency, with many nodes intended to reach Netflix, Disney+, TikTok, ChatGPT, and other services. Test the exact catalog region, AI feature, and account operation required.

### More than 60 locations

The advertised list includes Hong Kong, Japan, Singapore, the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, South Korea, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, and Turkey. It also claims more than 2 Gbps of spare capacity and independent IP resources.

Node presence, upstream capacity, and usable subscriber throughput are different facts. The source did not independently verify every location or capacity figure.

### Client compatibility

Provider materials say the service supports iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and routers through common clients. Confirm that the purchased tier exposes the exact subscription format needed and ask about simultaneous-device rules—the article's “unlimited devices” entry conflicts with its statement that device count depends on the plan.

### Support

The provider advertises online support, tickets, and an overseas technical team. Test the ticket system with a pre-purchase question and preserve the response.

## Before purchasing

1. Resolve the CNY 20 versus CNY 22 entry-price conflict at checkout.
2. Verify data, multiplier, reset date, device count, protocol, and refund policy.
3. Begin with the entry monthly tier rather than a high-value business plan.
4. Test evening-peak behavior, required regions, and exact streaming or AI features.
5. Keep a separately operated backup for work, livestreaming, stores, or important accounts.
6. Treat any “dedicated IP for account operations” claim as network information, not protection against platform enforcement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a beginner use it?

The monthly entry tier limits initial exposure. A beginner should still test the client format, local ISP, DNS, and intended service before considering a larger tier.

### Which plan is appropriate?

The 120 GB tier is the test option. Consider 300 GB only after measuring regular video or AI traffic. Plans at 700 GB and above make sense only when the route and service have already been validated.

### What matters most before checkout?

Check the current price, data allowance, node regions, device rules, reset method, support entry point, and whether the intended route works on the actual access carrier.

## Conclusion

Quanqiu Cloud presents a wide set of BGP and dedicated-route tiers, but most performance and service-access statements in the source come from provider materials. The sensible evaluation path is a small monthly purchase, repeated local tests, and an independent backup.
