## Free-tier boundaries

"Free, unlimited, permanent" does not explain:

- route capacity and peak-time queueing;
- acceptable-use and suspension thresholds;
- how the service is funded;
- data retention and sharing;
- device and concurrency rules; or
- whether a free route can disappear without compensation.

Do not use a free proxy for passwords, financial activity, confidential work, or valuable account administration without a security assessment.

## Gaming and streaming claims

The source claims optimization for Steam, PSN, Xbox, Netflix, YouTube, and Disney+. Gaming latency depends on the exact game server and route, while streaming access depends on the egress IP and region. Test each requirement rather than assuming the general label applies.

## TOKEN claim

Provider materials say use earns TOKEN that can be exchanged for renewal discounts or higher-tier access. No issuance formula, expiry, redemption table, transferability, financial value, or policy-change protection was supplied. Treat TOKEN as a revocable loyalty point, not an asset or guaranteed discount.

## FAQ

### Is the free tier truly permanent?

That is the provider's promise, not an independently enforceable guarantee. Verify current access and acceptable-use rules.

### What is the difference between VIP and SVIP?

The source labels SVIP as including a dedicated route. It does not provide a verifiable technical route specification.

### Which clients are supported?

The source lists major third-party client families on Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android. Confirm the live subscription format.

### Is the zero-log claim verified?

No. No independent audit or complete retention policy is provided in this article.
