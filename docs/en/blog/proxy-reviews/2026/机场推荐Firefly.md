## Provider information

The provider advertises VLESS over IPLC-labelled routes, native IP addresses, no speed or device limit, enterprise customization, and a website reachable without a proxy. It also claims access to YouTube, Netflix, Disney+, HBO, ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI, Claude, and Grok.

These are provider claims. Native-IP and IPLC labels are not standardized proof of route ownership, account safety, exclusivity, or continuing service access.

Confirm account expiry, inactivity, and closure rules. "No expiry" describes the allowance, not the operator.

## Universal-subscription limitation

The source says customers can request a standard subscription URL from support, but the dashboard did not yet expose a direct copy function. A buyer who requires Shadowrocket, Clash, or another third-party client should confirm the format and delivery process **before** purchase.

The provider also says devices and clients are unlimited, but no concurrency or acceptable-use policy was included.

## Purchase guidance

- Use Lite for one month.
- Confirm the subscription URL can be imported into your chosen client.
- Obtain concurrency and fair-use rules for multi-device or team use.
- Treat USDT as an irreversible payment.
- Test required regions and platforms during the evening peak.
- Obtain a written technical and support specification for any enterprise route.

## FAQ

### What is the lowest price?

The annual plan averages CNY 8 per month but costs CNY 96 up front. Lite is the lowest ordinary monthly tier at CNY 25.

### Is a universal subscription supported?

The source says support can provide one, but it was not directly copyable in the dashboard. Confirm before purchase.

### Are devices unlimited?

That is a provider claim. Concurrency and fair-use rules were not supplied.

### Is streaming and AI access verified?

No. The supplied image is a speed test, not a platform-access test.
