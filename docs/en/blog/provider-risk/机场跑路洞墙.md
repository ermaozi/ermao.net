## Evidence confirming the DlerCloud shutdown

### Website inaccessible

![DlerCloud website inaccessible =783x507](https://image.ermao.net/images/scamvpn/dlercloud/20260227_181242-97703d.png)

As of February 26, 2026, the DlerCloud website remained inaccessible. Users could not sign in, view plans, or obtain basic service information.

Online reports claimed that the operator had been arrested in mainland China, that the operator's Telegram account had been deleted, and that support was unreachable. These claims were circulating reports rather than independently verified facts.

The confirmed service interruption and inaccessible website were sufficient to classify the provider as shut down. Users were advised to:

- Stop making new purchases and renewals.
- Back up and migrate critical subscriptions immediately.
- Avoid adding a large amount of prepaid credit.

## Risk-response guidance

1. If any server still works, export the subscription and create a local backup first.
2. Move critical traffic to a backup route to reduce single-provider risk.
3. Preserve payment and communication records for any later dispute or claim.
