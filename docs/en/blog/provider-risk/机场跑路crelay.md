## Latest Crelay update (April 10, 2026)

According to the information available to the source article, Crelay was formerly known as Xrelay. After Xrelay closed, the service reappeared as Crelay and claimed to offer private routes. Users reported that the nodes became less stable after a price increase. These historical and route claims were not independently verified in the source.

## Analysis of Crelay's shutdown risk

::: caution

The available signals place Crelay at high risk of shutting down.

Stop purchasing and renewing.

:::

Website status at the time of the report: accessible.

---

### April 2026: nearly all nodes failed

Users reported that stable nodes were rarely available after April began. Many nodes showed errors and the service was largely unusable.

### Support tickets received no reply

Although the website could still be opened, submitted tickets received no visible reply or resolution while most nodes were failing.

![Unanswered Crelay support ticket](https://image.ermao.net/images/scamvpn/crelay/20260410_182643-af4d59.png)

## Conclusion

The current evidence shows:

- nearly all nodes showing errors and highly unstable service;
- support tickets left unanswered for an extended period;
- an accessible website that could still expose new purchasers to the unresolved service risk.

These conditions place Crelay at high risk of shutting down.

**High-risk alert:** Stop purchasing or renewing Crelay.
