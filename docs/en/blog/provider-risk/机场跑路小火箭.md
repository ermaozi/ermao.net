## Conclusion

The current evidence shows:

- widespread node errors and disconnections, with extremely high latency and severe stalling on the remaining routes;

These are typical shutdown warning signs and place Xiaohuojian in an extremely high-risk condition.

**High-risk alert:** Stop purchasing or renewing Xiaohuojian.
