## Latest Renzhe Cloud update (April 10, 2026)

## Analysis of the shutdown

::: caution

The combined evidence shows the conditions associated with a provider shutdown.

Stop purchasing and renewing.

:::

Reported website: https://renzhe.cloud

Telegram discussion group: No new messages had been posted for an extended period, and users could not establish effective communication.

---

### Nearly two months of unstable nodes

During the preceding two months, Renzhe Cloud's nodes became extremely unstable. Users reported that subscriptions could not be updated or used while their plans were still valid.

![Renzhe Cloud subscription failing to update =1440x3200](https://image.ermao.net/images/scamvpn/renzhe/20260410_180453-5b0061.png)

### April 10, 2026: support became unreachable

Users submitted tickets but received no response. Emails also went unanswered, while the Telegram notification channel had not been updated for a long time. The available official communication channels had therefore become ineffective.

![Unanswered Renzhe Cloud ticket, screenshot 1 =1440x3200](https://image.ermao.net/images/scamvpn/renzhe/20260410_180339-8d455a.png)

## Conclusion

The current evidence shows:

- almost two months of unstable nodes, followed by subscriptions that could not be updated;
- no replies through either tickets or email;
- a Telegram channel and notification group with no new updates for an extended period.

The source article classifies Renzhe Cloud as shut down on the basis of this combined service and communication failure.

**Do not purchase or renew Renzhe Cloud.**
