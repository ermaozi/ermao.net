Service has now been restored.

This page records an earlier XSUS shutdown risk alert.

During the alert period, the XSUS website was frequently inaccessible, customer support did not reply for an extended period, and its Telegram announcement channel had not posted for a long time.

These were high-risk signals. **The advice at the time was to stop renewing immediately.**
