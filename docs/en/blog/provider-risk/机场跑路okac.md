As of July 1, 2026, users had repeatedly reported route failures, inaccessible websites, and interrupted customer-support and management responses at OKAC. Because OKAC shares an operator with Naiyun, whose shutdown has been confirmed, this site currently classifies OKAC as a **provider shutdown risk**.

**Shutdown risk alert:** Existing users should immediately stop renewing or adding account credit. Preserve screenshots of payment records, support tickets, conversations, and subscription status first.

## Latest OKAC shutdown-risk update (July 1, 2026)

Websites reported during the incident included `www.okanc.com`, `https://www.okads.top/`, and `https://aff.okads.cc/`; users reported that none of them could be accessed normally. From mid-May through late June, the group continued to receive reports of nodes showing errors, unstable connections, and connections that appeared successful but were unusable.

An important connection is that **OKAC, Naiyun, CAC, and Yinyun share the same operator**. Naiyun's shutdown has been confirmed. OKAC's problems should therefore not be evaluated as an isolated website failure; the availability and communication status of CAC and Yinyun during the same period also matter.

::: warning Evidence limitations
The assessment below is compiled from available user reports and screenshots. Some details still require verification.

Until the platform provides a verifiable announcement and restores stable service, treat it as high risk.
:::

## Analysis of OKAC's shutdown risk

::: caution

OKAC has developed a combination of prolonged route failures, website-access problems, and interrupted management responses.

Stop renewing or purchasing immediately to avoid further losses.

:::

OKAC websites: Users reported that `www.okanc.com`, `https://www.okads.top/`, and `https://aff.okads.cc/` were all inaccessible.

---

- Website status: None of the reported domains—`www.okanc.com`, `https://www.okads.top/`, or `https://aff.okads.cc/`—could be accessed normally during the incident. Whether a temporary replacement domain exists remains unverified.
- Support status: Records show some conversations about restoring orders after mid-May, but replies later slowed significantly and then stopped. The exact time when support became fully unreachable remains unverified.
- Management status: No new, substantive recovery notice or follow-up explanation was seen after June 14.
- User reports: From mid-May through late June, users repeatedly reported nodes showing errors, unstable connections, and connections that succeeded but remained unusable.
- Operator connection: OKAC, Naiyun, CAC, and Yinyun share the same operator. Naiyun's confirmed shutdown increases the risk of correlated service and financial problems among the related projects.

## Conclusion

The available timeline and screenshots show the following high-risk characteristics:

- The A route failed repeatedly, while problems spread across multiple routes.
- Management acknowledged data loss and warned of a possible service interruption.
- The websites were inaccessible, and the existence of any temporary replacement domain remains unverified.
- OKAC shares an operator with Naiyun, whose shutdown has been confirmed, as well as with CAC and Yinyun; their risks should be evaluated together.
- In mid-to-late June, users reported a complete outage and ineffective communication.
- No visible, substantive recovery explanation was provided after June 14.

This site currently classifies OKAC as a **provider shutdown risk**.

There is not yet an explicit official closure notice or other evidence of an irreversible exit, so this page does **not** classify OKAC as a confirmed shutdown. The risk level may be raised if new evidence shows prolonged loss of contact, continued website unavailability, or funds that cannot be recovered.

## Loss-mitigation advice for existing users

- Stop renewing and adding account credit immediately.
- Back up and export subscription URLs, plan details, payment records, support tickets, and chat records.
- If support or the ticket system still accepts requests, submit a written refund or explanation request and take screenshots of every step.
- Move promptly to an alternative service whose current operating status can be independently checked, so that a single-provider outage does not disrupt all access.
