# About Ermao and This Site

> This is a personal technology and network-tools blog that is maintained over the long term. Its aim is to preserve information that can be checked, updated, and challenged—not merely advertisements from service providers.

::: card-grid cols="3"

- **🧪 Test whenever practical**

  Tutorials come from real installation and troubleshooting work. Reviews retain the test date, network, and device details whenever possible.

- **🔎 Identify sources**

  The site distinguishes its own tests, public third-party materials, provider claims, and editorial judgment.

- **📝 Accept corrections**

  When prices, versions, or service status change, readers are welcome to submit evidence that can be independently checked.

:::

## How content is produced

- Tutorials are based on actual installation, configuration, or troubleshooting and identify applicable versions whenever possible.
- Proxy-service reviews distinguish the site's own tests from provider disclosures and user reports.
- Plans, discounts, and server information are time-sensitive. Readers should verify them on the provider's own website before purchasing.
- Shutdown reports and risk alerts rely on observable facts. When evidence is incomplete, the article explicitly uses terms such as “suspected” or “risk alert” rather than presenting the claim as confirmed.
