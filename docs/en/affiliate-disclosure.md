# Affiliate and Commercial Disclosure

::: info In one sentence
Some links to proxy services, software, or other services contain affiliate parameters. If you register or make a purchase through one of these links, this site may receive a commission, normally without increasing the price you pay.
:::

::: card-grid cols="3"

- **💰 The site may earn a commission**

  Commercial relationships are disclosed instead of being disguised as ordinary citations.

- **⚖️ Rankings cannot be purchased**

  A commission must not change factual findings, risk notices, or negative status updates.

- **🛡️ No third-party guarantee**

  The provider remains responsible for its pricing, refunds, privacy practices, and operational risks.

:::

## What an affiliate relationship does not change

An affiliate relationship does not mean this site guarantees a third-party service. It must not affect factual descriptions, risk notices, or updates to negative information. If a service suffers a major outage, ceases operation, or shows another serious risk signal, the site will prioritize warning readers and may stop recommending it.

The site will not present unverified marketing claims as independently tested conclusions merely because a merchant paid for exposure. Free trials, test accounts, sponsorships, or other relationships that could affect judgment should also be disclosed on the relevant page.

::: warning Check before buying
Verify the current plans, refund policy, and terms of service, and favor a short billing period for an initial purchase. Neither historical performance nor a recommendation on this site eliminates the risks of relying on a third-party service.
:::
