# Editorial Policy

> This policy explains where an article's information comes from, how conclusions are reached, when content is updated, and how commercial relationships are kept separate from editorial judgment.

::: card-grid cols="3"

- **Facts first**

  Reproducible tests and primary materials take priority over promotional copy.

- **Clear boundaries**

  Provider claims and user reports are not presented as tests performed by this site.

- **Substantive updates**

  The modification date changes only when the content has actually changed.

:::

## Sources

The site prioritizes its own test results, official software documentation, public announcements, and primary materials that readers can check. Information supplied by a provider establishes only what that provider publicly claims; it does not automatically mean the claim has been independently verified.

::: card-grid cols="3"

- **🧪 Tests by this site**

  Results obtained by the site within the specified device, network, and time range.

- **📣 Official information**

  Provider or project pages, announcements, and support responses that may not be independently verifiable.

- **🧭 Editorial judgment**

  Analysis based on the available evidence, with uncertainty and the limits of applicability stated clearly.

:::

## Update principles

Prices, plans, software versions, availability, and streaming-unlock status can all change. A page's modification date is updated when its substance changes; formatting-only edits are not described as content updates. Outdated pages are revised, merged, redirected, or clearly marked as historical.

## Automation tools

The site may use automation to organize source material, check links, and improve structure, but publishable content must be reviewed by the site owner. Generated claims cannot replace test evidence and must not be described as first-hand testing.

## Conflicts of interest

Commercial relationships and affiliate commissions do not change the site's verification standards. Pages containing affiliate relationships must comply with the [Affiliate and Commercial Disclosure](/en/affiliate-disclosure/) and must also describe relevant risks and users for whom the service may be unsuitable.
